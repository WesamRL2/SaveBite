# SaveBite

SaveBite is a Java-based surplus food marketplace designed to reduce food waste and business losses.

Restaurants, cafes, bakeries, supermarkets, and other businesses can list surplus products at discounted prices near the end of the day.

Customers can reserve discounted products, helping businesses recover part of their costs while reducing unnecessary food waste.
